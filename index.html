Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about coaching, pricing, availability, and how 44 Physiques works.

How much does contest prep coaching cost?

Contest Prep is $275/month with a 3-month minimum ($825). A 6-month commitment is $1,650 and 12 months is $3,300.

Do you offer online coaching if I don’t live in Florida?

Yes. All coaching — nutrition, training, and weekly check-ins — is delivered fully online. Posing sessions are available via FaceTime for remote clients, or in-person for clients in Florida.

What is included in every 44 Physiques coaching package?

Every package includes a custom training program, personalized nutrition plan, weekly check-ins with real-time adjustments, and peak week guidance. Contest Prep and Rush Prep also include 2 posing sessions per month and a supplementation guide.

How do I start coaching with 44 Physiques?

Submit the inquiry form in the Contact section of the website. Describe your goals and timeline and you will receive a response within 48 hours to discuss the best coaching package.

What is Rush Prep and who is it for?

Rush Prep is a $425 flat-rate emergency prep package for competitors who are 4–5 weeks out from a show and need immediate, focused coaching. It includes everything in the standard Contest Prep package.

How are weekly check-ins done?

Each week clients submit front and back pose photos with good lighting. The coach reviews photos and data, then adjusts nutrition and training in real time based on progress and physique response.

What is Lifestyle Coaching at 44 Physiques?

Lifestyle Coaching is a $250/month program with a 3-month minimum designed for non-competitors seeking sustainable body composition improvement, health, and long-term habits through custom training and nutrition guidance.

What payment methods does 44 Physiques accept?

Zelle (preferred): 352-540-1695. PayPal: fentydavid@yahoo.com. Payments are due on the 1st or 15th of each month.
