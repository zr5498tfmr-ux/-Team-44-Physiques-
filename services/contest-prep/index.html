Questions

Contest Prep FAQ

Who is Contest Prep for?

Contest Prep is for bodybuilding, bikini, physique, and figure competitors preparing for a show who want precision nutrition, training, cardio, and posing built around their exact show date.

How much does Contest Prep cost?

Contest Prep is $275/month with a 3-month minimum ($825). A 6-month commitment is $1,650 and 12 months is $3,300.

Is Contest Prep available online?

Yes. All nutrition, training, and weekly check-ins are delivered fully online worldwide. Posing is available via FaceTime for remote clients or in-person for athletes in Florida.

What is included in Contest Prep?

Custom training, personalized nutrition, weekly check-ins with real-time adjustments, peak week guidance, 2 posing sessions per month, and a supplementation guide.
