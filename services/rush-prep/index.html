Who It's For

For Competitors Down to the Wire

Rush Prep exists for one situation: you're 4–5 weeks from stepping on stage and you need a sharp, experienced eye on your physique right now. Maybe a coaching situation fell through, maybe you decided late to compete, maybe your conditioning needs a fast, decisive plan.

This is the most hands-on window in the sport. Rush Prep compresses everything in standard Contest Prep into the final weeks—rapid structure, aggressive weekly adjustments, and a peak week plan that gets you stage-ready.

Questions

Rush Prep FAQ

Who is Rush Prep for?

Rush Prep is for competitors who are 4–5 weeks out from a show and need immediate, focused coaching with fast structure and detailed weekly adjustments.

How much is Rush Prep?

Rush Prep is a $425 flat rate, paid in full upfront.

What is included in Rush Prep?

Everything in the standard Contest Prep package: custom training, personalized nutrition, weekly check-ins with real-time adjustments, peak week guidance, 2 posing sessions per month, and a supplementation guide.

Is Rush Prep available online?

Yes. Coaching is delivered fully online worldwide. Posing is available via FaceTime for remote clients or in-person for athletes in Florida.
