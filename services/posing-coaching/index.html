Questions

Posing Coaching FAQ

Who is Posing Coaching for?

Posing Coaching is for competitors who want to improve stage presentation, confidence, transitions, mandatory poses, and overall competitive presence.

Is Posing Coaching available remotely?

Yes. Posing Coaching is available via FaceTime for remote clients and in-person for athletes located in Florida.

How much does Posing Coaching cost?

Posing Coaching is custom or session-based. It is also included as 2 posing sessions per month within Contest Prep and Rush Prep. Submit the inquiry form for standalone posing pricing.

What does Posing Coaching cover?

Stage presentation, confidence, transitions, mandatory poses, and overall competitive presence.
